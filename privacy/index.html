MassDomus Privacy Policy

Published by B2 Mobile Media INC (United States)

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the MassDomus mobile app ("MassDomus", "we", "us", "our") collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have. MassDomus is a directory that helps you find nearby Catholic parishes, Mass times, and confession schedules. The app is available in the United States and the Philippines.

The short version. You can browse MassDomus without an account and without giving us any personal information. An account is only required if you choose to subscribe to MassDomus Pro. We do not use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking technologies in this version of the app. Location is used only to find nearby parishes and is not stored.

1. Information we collect

Free use (no account)

You can use the core directory as a guest. In guest mode we do not ask you to sign in and we do not collect your name, email, or any account identifier.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app reads your device location to center the map and to search for parishes near you. The coordinates are sent to our parish directory service (hosted on Supabase) only to return nearby results. We do not store your location, and we do not tie it to your identity. Location is optional — if you decline, you can still search by city, state, or ZIP.

MassDomus Pro account (only if you subscribe)

To purchase or restore MassDomus Pro you sign in. Depending on the method you choose (Sign in with Apple or an email sign-in link), we may process:

  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Apple identity information — when you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides a secure identity token. Apple may share a real or private-relay email address depending on your Apple settings.
  • Name — if provided through Sign in with Apple, stored with your account profile.
  • Account identifier — a stable user ID that represents your account.

Sign-in and account data are handled by Supabase, our authentication and database provider.

Mass-time corrections (MassDomus Pro only)

If you are signed in with MassDomus Pro, you can send a correction to a parish's Mass or confession times. When you do, we store the correction on our servers (hosted on Supabase) and link it to your account. A correction contains the parish, the type of service, the day, the time, the language, any note you choose to add, its review status, the date it was sent, and your account identifier. Corrections are reviewed before they are shown in the directory. When an approved correction is shown to other users, your account identifier, name, and email are not shown with it.

Subscriptions and purchases

MassDomus Pro is a $20.00/month auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes your payment; we never receive or store your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage your subscription status and the "pro" entitlement. We provide RevenueCat only with your account identifier — we do not send your email address or name to RevenueCat.

Information stored only on your device

Some content never leaves your device: your saved favorites, display preferences, prayer intentions, rosary progress, and any community/prayer-board notes you create. These are stored locally in the app and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No analytics — the app contains no analytics SDK (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Amplitude, Segment, Mixpanel, or similar).
  • No crash/diagnostics reporting — no Sentry, Bugsnag, Crashlytics, or similar.
  • No push notifications — the app does not request push tokens.
  • No advertising at launch — this version does not display ads and does not use advertising identifiers (see "Advertising" below).
  • No tracking — we do not track you across apps or websites, and the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission or use your advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

3. How we use information

  • To show nearby parishes, Mass times, and confession schedules (location, transiently).
  • To create and secure your account and provide MassDomus Pro (email, Apple identity, name, account identifier).
  • To process and restore your subscription (account identifier via RevenueCat and Apple).
  • To review Mass-time corrections you send and, if approved, show the updated times in the directory (Pro only).
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for cross-app or cross-site advertising.

4. Advertising

This version of MassDomus does not serve advertisements and does not use advertising or tracking identifiers. We may add advertising (for example, Google AdMob) in a future update. If we do, we will update this Privacy Policy and present any required permission prompt before advertising is enabled.

5. Who we share information with

We share limited information only with service providers that help us run the app:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple and App Store In-App Purchase billing.
  • Supabase — account authentication and database hosting, the parish directory service, and storage of Mass-time corrections you send.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (receives your account identifier only).

We share this information only so these providers can perform their services for us. We only use service providers that are required to protect your information with the same or equal protection as described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

6. Data retention

Account information (such as your email and account identifier) and any Mass-time corrections you send are kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your account and associated server-side data (see "Your choices and account deletion"). On-device data is removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

When you delete your account, it is removed immediately from our live systems. Copies may remain for a limited time in routine backups kept by our hosting provider, Supabase; those backups expire on the provider's regular schedule.

7. Your choices and account deletion

  • Location: grant or deny location access in your device Settings at any time.
  • Sign out: in the app's Settings, "Sign Out" ends your session (it does not delete data).
  • Delete your account: if you are signed in (for example, as a MassDomus Pro subscriber), open the app's Settings, tap Delete Account, and confirm Permanently Delete Account. Your account and the data linked to it on our servers, including any Mass-time corrections you sent, are deleted immediately, and you are signed out. The app tells you when deletion is confirmed. If deletion cannot be confirmed, or you can no longer access the app, email us at apps@b2mobilemedia.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
  • Sign in with Apple: if you used Sign in with Apple, you can also stop using your Apple ID with MassDomus in your iPhone Settings: tap your name, then Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple › MassDomus.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw your consent at any time by turning off location access, signing out, or deleting your account as described above.
  • Subscriptions and on-device data are separate: deleting your account does not cancel an active Apple subscription (manage that in Apple Settings) and does not erase data saved on your device (delete the app or clear its data to remove that).

8. Children's privacy

MassDomus is a general-audience parish and Mass-time directory. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The free experience requires no account or personal information. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Account sign-in uses secure token-based authentication (PKCE), and session tokens are stored in the app's protected local storage. We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we process, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. International users

MassDomus is offered in the United States and the Philippines. Information may be processed and stored in the United States and other locations where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. We will update this policy before introducing any new category of data collection (such as advertising).
