Getting started

MassDomus is free to use — no account needed. Browse the directory, search by city/state/ZIP, and allow location access to find parishes near you (optional). An account is only required if you choose to subscribe to MassDomus Pro.

Location not working?

  • Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services and make sure Location Services is on.
  • Find MassDomus in the app list and allow location "While Using the App".
  • You can always search by city, state, or ZIP code if you prefer not to share location.

MassDomus Pro subscription

MassDomus Pro is a $20.00/month auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple account (Apple In-App Purchase). It renews automatically until you cancel. Sign-in is used only for Pro, so your subscription stays with your account across devices.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. To cancel or change your plan:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select MassDomus.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription (or change your plan).

If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the current billing period. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription — you must cancel through Apple Settings.

Restore purchases

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Settings inside MassDomus and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account & sign-in

You can sign in with Sign in with Apple or an email sign-in link. Sign-in is only needed for MassDomus Pro — free browsing never requires an account.

Sign out

In the app, open Settings › Account › Sign Out. Signing out ends your session but does not delete your account or data.

Delete your account and data

If you are signed in (for example, as a MassDomus Pro subscriber), you can delete your account right in the app:

  1. Open Settings inside MassDomus.
  2. Tap Delete Account, then Permanently Delete Account.

Your account and the data linked to it on our servers, including any Mass-time corrections you sent, are deleted immediately, and you are signed out. The app shows a message when deletion is confirmed.

If deletion cannot be confirmed, or you can no longer access the app, email apps@b2mobilemedia.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. Please note:

  • Deleting your account does not cancel an active Apple subscription — cancel that separately in Apple Settings (see above).
  • Data saved only on your device (favorites, preferences, prayer intentions, notes) is removed by deleting the app or clearing its data.
